9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access);
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • object to how we use it, including an absolute right to object to direct marketing (we’ll stop straight away);
  • receive your data in a commonly used format, or have it sent to another organisation (portability), where this applies;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To make a request, email [CONTACT_EMAIL] or write to us at the address above. We may need to confirm who you are. We’ll respond within one month, and tell you if a complex request needs longer. There’s usually no fee.
