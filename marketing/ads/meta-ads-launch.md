# BLACKLINE Meta ads: launch pack

Built from `brand.md` and the Meta ads playbook. UK market, so the ASA/CAP Code applies. Anything in [square brackets] is a figure or asset Safwan must supply or delete.

## Read first: proof and honesty notes

- There are no client results yet, so no ad uses testimonials, "we helped X", or results from named clients.
- Proof we can use: the live demo (always labelled a demo, fictional property management business), Safwan's own cold-call conversations (anonymised), and tests Safwan runs himself.
- The strongest honest proof is the mystery-shop test: send a test enquiry to [10-20] UK property managers' and dealerships' sites at 9pm and record who replies and how fast. Run it before launch. Every [N] in the ads below comes from it. If it isn't run, use the "demo" versions of the ads only.
- Don't show an AI-generated person as a customer or as Safwan. AI images are for scenes only (a phone on a desk, a quiet office at night).
- No guarantees. Don't promise more bookings or revenue.
- Price (£700 setup + £250/month) stays out of the ads.
- Confirm the demo replies in seconds by timing it yourself. Quote your own timing as [X seconds], or say "within a minute" only if that's what you measured.

## Offer and audiences

- **The click offer:** the live demo. "Send a test enquiry and watch it get answered, qualified and booked." Free, instant, checkable. A one-page leak checklist can be added later once it's built.
- **Audience A:** UK property managers.
- **Audience B:** UK car dealerships.
- **Audience C (test later):** mortgage brokers, clinics, recruitment agencies. Don't spend on it until A and B have data.

## Landing page and launch plan

**Landing pages:** one per audience, never the homepage. Mirror the winning ad headline in the page headline and subheadline.
- Page A: "What happens to a 9pm enquiry" for property managers.
- Page B: "Who replies first gets the test drive" for dealerships.
- Each page has one action: the demo form, then a thank-you page with the booking calendar. Label the demo as a demo, with a fictional business.

**Follow-up:** call or message every demo opt-in within the hour to confirm what they sent, ask what happens to their own enquiries after hours, and offer a call.

**Campaign setup:**
1. Testing campaign, campaign budget optimisation, objective Leads, broad targeting (UK, ages [25-65]), CTA Learn More.
2. One ad set per concept. Put 3-4 ads in each: different lead-ins, images and headlines.
3. Start at about [£20-30]/day with roughly 15-20 ad combinations in total.
4. Keep 10-20% of the budget for testing new creative.
5. Run 7 days without edits. Judge on cost per booked call first, then cost per demo lead, then click-through rate.
6. Set up the pixel and the Conversions API, and treat the CRM as the source of truth.
7. Retarget site visitors with the mystery-shop result, the demo screenshot and an objection-handling post.
8. Create each campaign **paused**. Nothing spends until Safwan switches it on.

**Weekly:** about 1 hour to make fresh creative for the best-performing concept (70% variations of winners, 20% adjacent, 10% wild).
