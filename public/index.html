    Good to know

    Can't find your answer? Ask us →

    Which tretinoin strength should I start with?
    Most people start with 0.025% and move up to 0.05% or 0.1% once their skin has adjusted, usually after a few months. Starting high tends to cause more irritation, not faster results.
    How long until I see results?
    Retinoids usually take 8–12 weeks of consistent use to show clear results. Some people go through a short "purging" phase in the first few weeks.
    Can I use these products together?
    Some combinations work well, such as azelaic acid in the morning and a retinoid at night. Others can irritate. Introduce one product at a time and check the leaflet, or ask a pharmacist.
