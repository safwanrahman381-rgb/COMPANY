# Working rules

## Models and usage
- Use `/model opusplan`: Opus plans, Sonnet executes.
- Delegate bulk or mechanical work (searching, scraping, drafting variations, repetitive edits) to Sonnet or Haiku subagents. Keep the main session for decisions.
- Don't spawn agents for tasks that take a few direct tool calls.

## Context
- Don't search past chats, old sessions or Notion/Drive history unless the task names something specific to find.
- Ask for missing inputs in one short question instead of hunting for them.
- Keep answers short. Put long deliverables in a doc, not in chat.

## Meta ads
- Follow the `meta-ads-playbook` skill for any ad, funnel or creative work.
- Never invent testimonials, results or numbers. Use [placeholders] for unknown figures.
- New campaigns launch paused. Ask before anything that spends money.
