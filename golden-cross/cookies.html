We don't use any non-essential cookies unless you say yes. You can change your choices at any time:

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as browser “local storage”, work in a similar way. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) we must ask for your consent before using any that aren't strictly necessary to provide the service you've asked for.

2. What we use

Maps (only with your consent)

Our “Find Us” section can show an interactive Google Map. The map is only loaded if you accept map cookies or click “Show map”. When loaded, Google may set cookies such as NID, AEC, SOCS and __Secure-ENID to remember preferences, prevent fraud and measure usage. These are controlled by Google — see Google's cookie policy.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If enabled, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit and which pages are useful. It sets _ga and _ga_<ID> cookies, which last up to 2 years. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4.

3. Managing your choices

Use the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page to change your choices at any time. If you withdraw consent, the page reloads and we stop loading the relevant services. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings — see the ICO's guide to cookies. Blocking strictly necessary storage may mean we ask for your cookie choice each time you visit.

4. More information

For how we handle personal data more generally, see our Privacy Policy. Questions? Call 01743 362507.
