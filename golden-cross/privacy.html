2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Booking and enquiry details — your name, email address, phone number, date and time, number of guests, room dates, and any message you send us.
  • Dietary, allergy and accessibility information — if you choose to tell us about allergies, dietary needs or access requirements. Some of this may be health information, which is a “special category” of data, so we only use it with your explicit consent and only to look after you during your visit.
  • Guest registration details — if you stay with us overnight, we are legally required to record your full name and nationality and, for guests who are not British, Irish or Commonwealth citizens, your passport number and place of issue and your next destination (Immigration (Hotel Records) Order 1972).
  • Payment information — if we take a deposit or payment, this is processed by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on this website.
  • Marketing preferences — if you tick the box to hear from us.
  • Correspondence — emails, messages, reviews and feedback you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data — our hosting provider automatically logs basic information such as your IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — only strictly necessary storage is used unless you agree otherwise. See our Cookie Policy.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We only share it with trusted service providers who help us run our business, under contracts that require them to keep it safe and use it only on our instructions:

  • our website host and form-handling provider ;
  • our email and booking-management providers;
  • our payment provider, where a deposit or payment is taken;
  • Google, only if you consent to maps or analytics;
  • our professional advisers (such as accountants and lawyers), insurers and IT support;
  • the police, HMRC, the Home Office or other authorities where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some of our service providers may store data outside the UK. Where this happens we make sure your data is protected — for example by using countries the UK has recognised as providing adequate protection (including certified organisations under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or by using the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data;
  • object to us using your data where we rely on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • data portability — receive a copy of data you gave us in a commonly used format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent (this won't affect anything we did before).

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. It's free, and we will reply within one month. We may need to check your identity first. Some rights have exceptions — for example, we must keep certain records by law.

8. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

9. Keeping your data safe

This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We limit access to personal data to people who need it, use reputable providers with strong security, and have procedures to deal with any suspected data breach, including telling you and the ICO where we are legally required to.

10. Children

Our website and booking service are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 through this website.

11. Other websites

Our site links to other websites, such as Google Maps and Google reviews. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
