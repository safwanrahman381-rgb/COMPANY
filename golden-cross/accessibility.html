Accessibility

Everyone should be able to enjoy The Golden Cross — online and in person.

On this page

  1. 1. Visiting us
  2. 2. This website
  3. 3. Known limitations
  4. 4. Feedback

1. Visiting us

  • We have a wheelchair-accessible entrance.
  • We're in a characterful historic building, which means some areas have uneven floors, steps or narrow spaces. Please call ahead on 01743 362507 and we'll make sure you have the most comfortable table and any help you need.
  • Assistance dogs are always welcome.
  • If you have any access needs, tell us in the notes when you book and we'll make reasonable adjustments, as we're happy to do (and as the Equality Act 2010 requires).

2. This website

We want this website to be usable by as many people as possible. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The site:

  • works with screen readers and keyboard-only navigation, with a “skip to content” link and visible focus outlines;
  • uses readable colour contrast and text that can be resized up to 200% without loss of content;
  • respects your device's “reduce motion” setting by turning off animations;
  • works on mobiles, tablets and desktops.

3. Known limitations

  • The embedded Google Map is provided by a third party and may not be fully accessible. Our address and a direct link to directions are always shown as text.
  • Our menu changes regularly; if a menu is ever provided as an image or PDF, we'll give you the same information by phone or email on request.

4. Feedback

If you find anything on this website hard to use, or you need information in a different format, please call 01743 362507 and we'll help. We review this statement regularly.
