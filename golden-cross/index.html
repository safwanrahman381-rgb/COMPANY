Our Story

Tucked away.
Worth the detour.

Most of our guests find us by chance — wandering down the flower-lined passage off Princess Street — and come back on purpose. Quirky, characterful and a little shabby-chic, The Golden Cross is where Shrewsbury comes for food that's made with real care.

  • An ever-changing menu

    Fresh, seasonal cooking that shifts with what's good right now — so there's always something new to try.

  • A wine list worth lingering over

    Bubbles, rosé, reds, whites and a list of stickies to finish — chosen to go with the food.

  • Friendly, never pretentious

    Owner-run with a genuinely warm welcome — whether it's a quick lunch or a long celebration dinner.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need to book a table?

We always try to fit in walk-ins, but we're a cosy spot and weekends fill up fast — booking is the best way to guarantee a table. Book online or call 01743 362507.

Can you cater for allergies and dietary requirements?

Yes. Please tell us about any allergies or dietary needs when you book and again when you order. Information on the 14 major allergens is available for every dish — see our allergen information.

Is The Golden Cross wheelchair accessible?

We have a wheelchair-accessible entrance. As we're in a historic building, please call ahead so we can seat you in the most comfortable spot.

Do you have rooms?

Yes. Send us your dates using the room enquiry form or call us, and we'll confirm availability and rates.

Where can I park?

We're in Shrewsbury town centre, a short walk from several public car parks and the Park & Ride. Give us a call and we'll point you to the most convenient option.

Can I bring a group or celebrate a special occasion?

We'd love that. For groups of 9 or more, please call us so we can plan the perfect table. Mention your occasion when you book and we'll do our best to make it memorable.
