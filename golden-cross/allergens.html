Allergen Information

Your safety matters to us. Here's how we handle allergies and dietary requirements.

On this page

  1. 1. The 14 major allergens
  2. 2. Cross-contamination

Please speak to a member of our team before you order if you have a food allergy or intolerance. Full, up-to-date allergen information for every dish on today's menu is available from our team, in writing, on request.

1. The 14 major allergens

Under the Food Information Regulations 2014, we can tell you if any of our dishes contain the following allergens as ingredients:

  • Celeryincluding celeriac
  • Cereals containing glutenwheat, rye, barley, oats
  • Crustaceanse.g. crab, prawns
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Lupin
  • Milk
  • Molluscse.g. mussels, squid
  • Mustard
  • Tree nutse.g. almonds, walnuts
  • Peanuts
  • Sesame
  • Soya
  • Sulphur dioxide / sulphitesoften in wine & dried fruit

2. Cross-contamination

Our menu changes regularly and all our dishes are prepared in the same kitchen, where all 14 allergens are handled. We take great care, but we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from traces of allergens. Fried items may share the same fryer.

3. Vegetarian, vegan and other diets

We're happy to help with vegetarian, vegan and other dietary preferences wherever we can. Let us know when you book — using the notes box on our booking form — and remind your server when you order.

4. Drinks

Many wines and some other drinks contain sulphites, and some may be fined using egg, milk or fish products. Please ask if you need details for a particular drink.

5. Questions?

Call us on 01743 362507 before your visit and we'll talk you through the options. For more on food allergies, see the Food Standards Agency.
