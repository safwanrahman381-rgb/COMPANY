# BLACKLINE brand context

## Who and what

- **Brand:** BLACKLINE (blacklinehq.co.uk). It's a trading name of Safwan Rahman, based in South Wales and serving the whole UK.
- **Line:** "We don't sell AI. We build systems that use it."
- **Offer:**
    - The system sits behind the business's existing website and forms. No rebuild.
    - Every enquiry is answered within seconds, qualified, and booked into the calendar.
    - It's logged in the CRM and followed up automatically, with a human handoff when needed.
    - Flagship service: Lead to Booking, plus an AI Agent.
- **Price:** founding-client pricing of £700 setup + £250/month. Only mention it if Safwan asks.
- **Proof status:** no client results yet. Proof that can be used:
    - the live demo on the site (a fictional property management business);
    - the BLACKLINE Demo pipeline in GoHighLevel;
    - Safwan's own cold-call conversations;
    - any real test Safwan runs himself.

## Contrarian vault

These are seed positions taken from Safwan's own site positioning. He must confirm or edit each one, and Claude never invents new ones without asking.

- Most businesses don't need more leads. They need to answer the ones they already get.
- A chatbot on its own isn't a system. If it can't check the calendar, update the CRM and hand off to a human, it's a toy.
- You don't need a new website to fix this. The problem is what happens after the form.
- Speed beats polish: a fast, plain reply wins the viewing or test drive over a perfect reply the next morning.

**Words to own:** "after the form", "every enquiry answered", "systems, not AI".

## Voice

- Direct, plain UK English, written the way Safwan talks.
- Young founder who cold calls businesses himself. Honest about being early: "I'm building this", never "we've helped hundreds".
- Specific over clever. Uses real details from calls and builds.
- Calls the niche "property managers", never "letting agents".
- No hype words and no em dashes. 0-2 emojis.

## Visual house style (for `render_slides.py`)

- Black background, white text, a single electric-blue accent line (#2F6BFF), and a heavy sans font.
- Small footer: "Safwan · BLACKLINE". No big logos.
- Screenshots and raw photos are kept unedited and only cropped.
