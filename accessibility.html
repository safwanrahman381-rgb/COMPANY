Accessibility statement

Last updated: [DATE]

This statement covers the BLACKLINE website at [DOMAIN].

Our aim

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we’ve done

  • Keyboard navigation. Everything can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Reduced motion. If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are switched off and the scroll-driven scenes show their finished state.
  • Colour contrast. Text and controls are chosen to stand out clearly against the dark background.
  • Semantic structure. Pages use proper headings, landmarks and lists, so they can be navigated with a screen reader.
  • Screen reader labels. Buttons, tabs and form fields are labelled, form errors are announced, and demo updates are read out as they happen.

Known limitations

Some animated demos, such as the network in the opening section, the “From chaos to system” scene and the service animations, are mainly visual and may be harder to use with assistive technology. The same information is available as text on the page: in the service descriptions, the “What we fix” step lists and the demo step lists.

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in a different format such as large print or plain text, email [CONTACT_EMAIL]. Please tell us which page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within [5] working days.
